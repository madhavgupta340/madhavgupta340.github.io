I’m Madhav, I’m 19, and I’m in my first semester of physics at the Technical University of Munich.

## Physics

Physics is the main thing right now, and it takes up most of my week: lectures, problem sheets, and slowly working out how things actually behave underneath. First semester is all foundations, and there’s a lot of it, but that’s exactly what I signed up for.

## AI and new tech

The other thing I can’t stay away from is making stuff with AI and new tech. When something new comes out, I want to figure out what it can really do and build something with it.

- [ChainBridge]({{ '/projects/chainbridge/' | relative_url }}) came out of the TUM Blockchain & AI Hackathon: an AI agent that reads documents, picks out the facts that matter, and seals a fingerprint of them on the Cardano blockchain, with pay-per-use payments built in.
- [PaperTrade]({{ '/projects/papertrade/' | relative_url }}) is a stock-trading simulator: pretend money, real tickers, live or demo prices, and a proper order system behind it.
